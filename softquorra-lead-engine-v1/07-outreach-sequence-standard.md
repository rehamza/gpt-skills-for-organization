# Outreach Sequence Standard
Message 1:
reason for outreach + problem/outcome + relevant proof/mechanism + simple CTA.

Follow-up 1:
add new context.

Follow-up 2:
new proof/resource/angle.

Close-out:
respectfully close the loop.

Stop/pause on:
unsubscribe/stop, explicit no, timing instruction, or channel/legal constraint.

No fake urgency, fake referrals, deceptive subjects, or unsupported ROI.

# Outbound Safety & Brand
Use truthful identity, relevant business purpose, public/authorized data, reasonable volume, and explicit stop handling.

Do not:
- scrape private/restricted data
- bypass anti-bot/platform controls
- evade rate limits
- conceal sender identity
- impersonate
- fabricate referrals/pain/events
- guess private contact data
- continue after stop/unsubscribe
- use discriminatory/sensitive targeting

Use human review for new campaigns, high-value accounts, new claims, high-volume automation, sensitive industries, and ambiguous replies.

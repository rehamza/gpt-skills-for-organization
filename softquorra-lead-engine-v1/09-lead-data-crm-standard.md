# Lead Data & CRM Standard
Track:
Account: company/domain/industry/geography/qualification/evidence/trigger/campaign.
Contact: verified name/role/source/relevance/contactability.
Research: signal/source/date/fact-vs-inference.
Outreach: campaign/message/date/channel/status.
Reply: class/date/next action/timing/stop state.
Pipeline: qualified/conversation/meeting/opportunity/won/lost where applicable.

Store only useful business data and preserve source provenance.

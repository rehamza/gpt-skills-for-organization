# Account Qualification
For each account assess firmographic fit, workflow fit, trigger, buyer, commercial fit, and exclusions.
Classify QUALIFIED / POSSIBLE / DISQUALIFIED / INSUFFICIENT DATA.
Output evidence and concerns.
Do not invent pain, funding, revenue, employee count, tech stack, or intent.

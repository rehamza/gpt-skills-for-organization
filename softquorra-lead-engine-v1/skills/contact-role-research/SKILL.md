# Contact Role Research
Determine who owns the problem, uses the solution, approves budget, and evaluates technical/security concerns.
Use public professional sources for verified role holders.
Output primary role, why, verified contact/source if available, alternate role, contactability.
Do not guess emails/phones or infer sensitive traits.

# Lead Campaign Review
Review data completeness, qualified-account rate, disqualification reasons, correct-role coverage, reply quality, meetings/opportunities, pipeline, objections, and stop rate.
Compare segments, triggers, buyer roles, messages, offers, and channels.
Recommend targeting/query/message/offer/channel changes and Growth Feedback.
Do not optimize for send volume alone.

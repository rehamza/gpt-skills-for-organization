# SoftQuorra Lead Context
SoftQuorra is a software and AI development company plus product studio.

Approved campaigns may support:
- AI agents and workflow automation
- SaaS/product development
- web/mobile applications
- business systems
- integrations
- dedicated developers
- product pods/full delivery teams
- internal products such as Socialope

Each campaign should have one clear ICP, buyer, problem, offer, and CTA.

Avoid generic “we build software” outreach.

Do not invent client results, logos, case studies, delivery timelines, or ROI.

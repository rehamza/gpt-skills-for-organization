# SoftQuorra QA Context

SoftQuorra ships:
- SaaS/web/mobile products;
- AI agents/workflows;
- integrations;
- business systems;
- client software;
- internal products such as Socialope.

QA should protect:
- approved product behavior;
- tenant/customer data;
- billing/entitlements;
- critical integrations;
- AI reliability;
- backward compatibility;
- production operability.

Quality is not “no bugs.”
Quality means material user/business/security risks are understood, tested, and acceptable for the release.

# Cross-GPT Security & Compliance Handoffs

## Product Architect
Provide risk, affected product/user/data, source, requirement IDs, disclosures/choice, acceptance criteria, vendor constraints, retention/deletion, rights/incident handling and open legal decisions.

## Engineering Planner
Provide approved IDs, control objective, data/architecture context, constraints, evidence/testing, migration/rollout, logging/monitoring, dependencies, residual risk and ADR need.

## Codex / Claude
Provide bounded fix: defect/risk, requirement, repo context, evidence/reproduction, expected secure behavior, tests, non-goals, validation and stop conditions. Do not send unnecessary legal/confidential/personal records.

## QA
Provide security/privacy requirements, threat cases, tenant/auth cases, integrations, deletion/retention, audit/log expectations, AI cases, blockers and required evidence.

## Growth
Provide approved/prohibited claims, evidence limits, privacy/cookie/disclosure needs, testimonial/logo permissions and security-positioning status.

## Lead Engine
Provide approved audience/channel, data-source restrictions, personalization restrictions, legal sender, opt-out/suppression, prohibited claims, retention and review conditions.

## External Professionals
Provide facts, unknowns, documents, specific questions, options, impact, verified deadlines and management decision needed.

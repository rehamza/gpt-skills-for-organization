# Security Governance Framework

Use NIST CSF 2.0 as the high-level structure:

## Govern
Accountable owner, risk appetite, policies, asset/data owners, supplier risk, access approvals, metrics, obligation register, management decisions and accepted risk.

## Identify
Asset/data/system/vendor inventories, data flows, threat models, dependency inventory, business impact, critical services and risk register.

## Protect
MFA/passkeys, password manager, least privilege, identity lifecycle, encryption, secrets, tenant isolation, secure development, endpoint controls, backups, awareness, minimization and retention/deletion.

## Detect
Auth/admin logs, audit events, monitoring, alerting, suspicious-access detection, secret/dependency scanning and incident reporting.

## Respond
Incident lead, severity, containment, evidence, communications, counsel/insurer/vendor escalation and notification assessment.

## Recover
Tested backups/restores, recovery priorities, rollback/forward-fix, support communications and lessons learned.

Every risk record includes ID, scope, threat, weakness, likelihood, impact, owner, treatment, evidence, due date, residual risk and approval.

Use current official versions of NIST CSF, the final NIST Privacy Framework, OWASP ASVS and CISA secure-by-design guidance. Framework use does not prove certification.

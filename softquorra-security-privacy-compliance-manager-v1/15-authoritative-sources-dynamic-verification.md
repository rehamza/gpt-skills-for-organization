https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business

For each market/product/campaign, research current official sources for privacy, electronic marketing, cookies, consumer protection, breach notification, children, employment, payments/tax, export/sanctions, AI rules, sector rules and platform/API policies.

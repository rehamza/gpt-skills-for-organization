# Incident Response & Business Continuity Standard

Potential incidents: unauthorized/cross-tenant access, token compromise, data loss, malware, vendor breach/outage, fraud, source-code leak, ransomware, takeover, unauthorized publishing/outreach, AI tool misuse or major availability failure.

First actions:
1. protect people/critical operations;
2. open incident record and assign lead;
3. preserve evidence/timeline;
4. limit access/disclosure;
5. contain safely;
6. identify systems/data/users/vendors;
7. engage engineering, management, vendor, counsel and insurer as needed;
8. assess notification duties with counsel;
9. recover and verify;
10. complete lessons learned.

Record ID, detection time, reporter, lead, systems, facts/assumptions, timeline, containment, data/users, vendors, communications, legal assessment, recovery, root cause and corrective actions.

Only authorized people communicate externally. Do not speculate, minimize or destroy evidence.

Continuity requires critical-service priorities, owners, dependencies, backups/restores, alternate communications, emergency access, manual workarounds, recovery objectives and customer support.

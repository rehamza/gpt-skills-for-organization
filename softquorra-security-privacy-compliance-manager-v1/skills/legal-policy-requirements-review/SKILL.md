# Legal & Policy Requirements Review

1. Identify entity, activity, parties and jurisdictions.
2. Confirm company/contract facts.
3. Identify required/affected documents.
4. Map data, IP, security, payment, liability, support, termination, incident and publicity questions.
5. Identify official legal/regulator sources.
6. Separate internal policy vs customer-facing terms.
7. Identify approvers/counsel questions.
8. Produce document plan.

All language is DRAFT FOR QUALIFIED LEGAL REVIEW. Do not choose governing law, accept liability or sign commitments.

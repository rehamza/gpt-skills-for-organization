# Incident Response Coordinator

1. Confirm safe channel and incident lead.
2. Record detection/time/systems/facts.
3. Preserve evidence/timeline.
4. Assess active harm and containment.
5. Identify data/users/vendors.
6. Assign provisional severity.
7. Coordinate engineering, management, vendor, legal, insurer and support.
8. Identify notification questions for counsel.
9. Define recovery/verification.
10. Create corrective actions/lessons.

Do not destroy evidence, conceal incidents or speculate externally.

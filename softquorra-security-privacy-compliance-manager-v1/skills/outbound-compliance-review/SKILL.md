# Outbound Compliance Review

1. Identify sender/entity, campaign, audience and channel.
2. Identify jurisdictions/current requirements to research.
3. Review contact data and personalization.
4. Review claims/evidence.
5. Review sender identity, address/disclosure and opt-out questions.
6. Review suppression/stop propagation.
7. Review platform/vendor responsibilities.
8. Review automation volume/human approval.
9. Return changes/status.

Do not approve deceptive claims, hidden opt-outs, guessed private contacts, platform evasion or contact after stop.

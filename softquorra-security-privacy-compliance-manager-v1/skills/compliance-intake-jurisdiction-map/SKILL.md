# Compliance Intake & Jurisdiction Map

1. Identify request, decision and deadline.
2. Identify entity, product/service and authoritative records.
3. Identify customers/users/data subjects.
4. Identify countries/states/contract jurisdictions.
5. Identify data, vendors and systems.
6. Classify corporate/contract/privacy/security/vendor/incident/AI/outbound/certification/tax issue.
7. Separate facts, assumptions and unknowns.
8. List official sources/professionals needed.
9. Define owners and GPT handoffs.
10. Return readiness/escalation.

Output: decision, scope, facts, jurisdictions, data/parties, obligations to research, evidence, gaps, owners, handoffs and status.

Do not infer formation jurisdiction, duties or deadlines; do not request unnecessary sensitive records.

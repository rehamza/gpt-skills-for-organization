# Privacy Role & DPIA Assessment

1. Select one processing activity.
2. Identify who determines purpose and essential means.
3. Identify instructions and independent decisions.
4. Map possible roles by party.
5. Identify jurisdictions/current official guidance.
6. Screen sensitive data, children, monitoring, profiling, consequential automation, scale, novel AI, location/biometrics and other high-risk factors.
7. Identify notice, choice, rights, transfer, retention and DPA questions.
8. Define mitigations and counsel questions.
9. Return provisional status.

Label role provisional until qualified legal review.

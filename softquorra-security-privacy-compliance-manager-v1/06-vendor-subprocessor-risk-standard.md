# Vendor & Subprocessor Risk Standard

Review vendors affecting personal/customer data, source code, auth, payments, communications, analytics, AI, hosting, monitoring, support or continuity.

Record vendor/legal name, service, owner, product, data/access, purpose, locations/transfers, subprocessors, retention/deletion, AI training/data use, security evidence, audits/certifications, incident terms, availability, contract/DPA, exit/portability, concentration risk and review date.

Evidence may include signed contracts/DPAs, security docs, current scoped audit reports, penetration-test summaries, certifications, subprocessor lists, privacy terms, deletion commitments and continuity information.

A badge or marketing page alone is not sufficient for critical vendors.

AI vendors also require review of prompts/files, retention, provider training, human review, API/enterprise terms, regions, model changes, abuse logs, deletion and service continuity.

Decision: APPROVED / APPROVED WITH CONDITIONS / PILOT ONLY / BLOCKED / EXTERNAL REVIEW REQUIRED.

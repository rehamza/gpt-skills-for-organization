# Contract, Policy & Legal-Document Requirements Standard

Identify required artifacts and legal-review questions; do not authorize final wording or signature.

Company-level possibilities: corporate authority records, employee/contractor IP/confidentiality, MSA/SOW, vendor agreements, DPA, security policies, incident response, access control, vendor risk, retention and continuity.

Product trust possibilities: Terms, Privacy Policy, Cookie Notice, Acceptable Use Policy, DPA, Subprocessor List, Security Overview, retention/deletion statement, rights request method, incident process, billing/cancellation/refund and support/SLA terms.

Review entity, scope, IP, confidentiality, data roles, security, subprocessors, incident notice, warranties, SLAs, payment/tax, liability, indemnity, termination, data return/deletion, audit, governing law, publicity and signer authority.

Generated legal drafts must say:
`DRAFT FOR QUALIFIED LEGAL REVIEW — NOT APPROVED OR EXECUTED.`

Do not claim “fully compliant,” “100% secure,” “GDPR certified,” “SOC 2 compliant” or “HIPAA compliant” without valid current evidence and authority.

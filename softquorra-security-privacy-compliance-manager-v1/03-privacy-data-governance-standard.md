# Privacy & Data Governance Standard

For every processing activity record:
- system/activity and owner;
- data subjects/categories/source/purpose;
- required vs optional data;
- systems/vendors/locations/transfers;
- access roles;
- retention/deletion/backups;
- security controls;
- rights-request and incident impact;
- controller/processor questions;
- legal-review owner.

Do not classify SoftQuorra globally. Determine roles per activity based on who decides purposes and essential means.

Socialope activities needing separate analysis include account/billing, support, analytics, social tokens/data, customer content, leads/contacts, outreach, AI prompts/outputs and security logs.

Use data minimization, transparency, accuracy, retention limitation, access control, security, accountability and user rights where applicable.

Do not invent a lawful basis, consent requirement or legal deadline.

Escalate for DPIA/high-risk review where sensitive data, children, systematic monitoring, profiling, consequential automation, novel AI, precise location, biometrics, employee monitoring or high-risk combinations are involved.

Every retained data category needs a reason, duration/rule, deletion/anonymization method, backup treatment, legal-hold exception, owner and evidence.

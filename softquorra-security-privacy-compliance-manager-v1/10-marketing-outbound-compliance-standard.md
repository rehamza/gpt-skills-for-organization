# Marketing & Outbound Compliance Standard

Review email, professional social outreach, forms, newsletters, ads, cookies/tracking, lead databases, affiliates, testimonials, comparisons and automated campaigns.

Record sender/legal entity, offer/audience, jurisdiction/channel, contact source, purpose, claims/proof, personalization data, sender identity, address/disclosure question, opt-out, suppression, vendor/platform, retention and owner.

Claims must be truthful, evidence-backed, scoped, current and authorized. Never fabricate customers, logos, reviews, revenue, certifications, features, ROI, scarcity or referrals.

Explicit stop/unsubscribe must halt outreach, be recorded, propagate to systems/vendors and prevent re-import.

Do not scrape private/restricted data, bypass access controls, buy data without review, infer sensitive traits, guess private contact details or evade platform limits.

For US commercial email, review current FTC CAN-SPAM guidance. For UK/EEA/other markets, review current data-protection and electronic-marketing rules with counsel. US compliance is not global compliance.

Before Socialope automation verify sender, contact source, channel permissions, suppression, limits, approvals, records, vendor responsibilities and stop routing.

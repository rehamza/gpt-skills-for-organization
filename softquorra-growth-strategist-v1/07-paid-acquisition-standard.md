# Paid Acquisition Standard

## Paid Readiness Gate
Do not scale paid spend until:
- ICP is defined;
- offer is understandable;
- landing/conversion path works;
- tracking is reliable;
- basic funnel conversion is known or being tested;
- budget and stop rules exist.

## Campaign Brief
- objective;
- audience;
- platform;
- offer;
- message;
- landing page;
- conversion event;
- budget;
- duration;
- creatives;
- exclusions;
- primary metric;
- guardrail metric.

## Creative Tests
Test one or two major variables at a time:
- pain;
- outcome;
- proof;
- format;
- CTA;
- audience.

## Metrics
Depending on motion:
- CPM;
- CTR;
- CPC;
- landing conversion;
- lead/signup;
- qualification;
- activation;
- CAC;
- pipeline;
- revenue;
- retention.

Do not optimize only for cheap clicks/leads.

## Budget
Use:
- test budget;
- scale condition;
- stop condition;
- max loss acceptable for learning.

Never fabricate a “correct” ad budget without context.

## Tracking
Define:
- campaign/UTM taxonomy;
- conversion events;
- source attribution;
- CRM/product linkage;
- duplicate handling;
- privacy/platform constraints.

## Scale Decision
Scale when:
- signal is repeatable;
- lead/user quality is acceptable;
- downstream economics support it;
- creative fatigue is manageable;
- measurement is trusted.

## Stop/Pivot
Stop or revise when:
- wrong audience;
- weak offer;
- landing failure;
- poor downstream quality;
- cost exceeds plausible economics;
- policy/reputation risk.

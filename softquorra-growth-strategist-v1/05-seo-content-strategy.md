# SEO & Content Strategy

## Principle
SEO is a demand-capture and education system, not a word-count factory.

## Search Intent Lanes
Research:
1. Problem intent
2. Category intent
3. Use-case intent
4. Comparison/alternative intent
5. Integration intent
6. Commercial/service intent
7. Educational authority
8. Brand/navigation

## Topic Selection
For each topic capture:
- query/topic;
- intent;
- ICP;
- buying stage;
- current SERP type;
- competitor coverage;
- SoftQuorra/product advantage;
- conversion destination;
- internal links;
- proof/data needed.

## Content Types
- service page;
- solution/use-case page;
- comparison page;
- alternative page;
- integration page;
- technical guide;
- tutorial;
- case study;
- research/data;
- template/checklist;
- product documentation;
- FAQ.

## Content Brief
Should include:
- target intent;
- audience;
- promise;
- search questions;
- angle;
- evidence;
- outline;
- CTA;
- internal links;
- claims to verify;
- differentiation.

## Quality Rules
- satisfy the query;
- demonstrate real expertise;
- be original;
- avoid keyword stuffing;
- avoid fake experience;
- cite current facts;
- separate opinion from evidence;
- avoid publishing many near-duplicate pages.

## Measurement
Track:
- indexed/eligible pages;
- qualified organic visits;
- query/page movement;
- conversion;
- activation/pipeline from organic;
- assisted conversion.

Rankings alone are not the business result.

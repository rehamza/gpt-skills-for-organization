# Weekly Growth Digest

## Inputs
Use available:
- primary outcome metric;
- funnel metrics;
- channel metrics;
- experiment results;
- pipeline/revenue;
- activation/retention;
- spend;
- user/prospect feedback;
- incidents/data-quality notes.

Do not invent missing data.

## Workflow
1. State data period and completeness.
2. Compare primary outcome to prior period/baseline where valid.
3. Identify meaningful funnel movement.
4. Rank channel quality, not reach alone.
5. Summarize experiments: win/loss/inconclusive.
6. Extract customer/prospect insights.
7. Identify risks/anomalies.
8. Recommend 1–3 next decisions.
9. Separate measurement problems from performance problems.

## Output
- Executive summary
- Primary outcome
- Funnel
- Channel insights
- Experiments
- Qualitative insights
- Risks/data quality
- Decisions
- Next-week priorities

## Quality Check
Do not claim causation from correlation. Do not celebrate vanity metrics without downstream impact.

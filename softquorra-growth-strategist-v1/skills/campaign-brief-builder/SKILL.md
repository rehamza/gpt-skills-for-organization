# Campaign Brief Builder

## Inputs
Collect or infer only when supported:
- product/service;
- commercial objective;
- ICP/buyer;
- problem/trigger;
- offer;
- positioning;
- proof;
- channel(s);
- budget/timeline;
- CTA;
- restrictions;
- available assets.

Mark missing critical inputs.

## Workflow
1. Restate the business objective.
2. Define primary audience and exclusion.
3. Define one campaign promise/message.
4. Choose offer and CTA.
5. Choose channel role.
6. Define required creative/assets.
7. Define funnel destination.
8. Define tracking and primary metric.
9. Define guardrail metric.
10. Define test variants.
11. Define launch/stop/scale criteria.
12. Run claims check.

## Output
- Campaign name
- Objective
- ICP
- Insight/problem
- Message
- Offer
- CTA
- Channels
- Asset list
- Funnel
- Tracking
- Test plan
- Success/stop rules
- Risks
- Open questions

## Quality Check
Do not finalize if:
- ICP is “everyone”;
- CTA does not match buying stage;
- success is only impressions;
- factual claims are unsupported;
- the campaign requires an unapproved product feature.

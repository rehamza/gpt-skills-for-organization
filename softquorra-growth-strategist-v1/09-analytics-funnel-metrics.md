# Growth Analytics & Funnel Metrics

## Principle
Choose metrics from the business model and customer journey.

## Common Funnel

### Acquisition
- qualified impressions/reach;
- visits;
- source/channel;
- cost.

### Conversion
- signup;
- lead;
- demo;
- trial;
- contact.

### Activation
The first meaningful value event.

Define product-specific activation; do not default to signup.

### Retention
- return/use;
- cohort retention;
- active account;
- repeated value event.

### Monetization
- trial-to-paid;
- paid conversion;
- MRR/ARR where relevant;
- expansion;
- churn;
- contribution economics.

### B2B Pipeline
- MQL only if useful;
- qualified lead;
- meeting;
- opportunity;
- proposal;
- win;
- contract value;
- sales cycle.

## Metric Definitions
For every important metric define:
- formula;
- event/source;
- window;
- segment;
- owner.

## Attribution
Treat attribution as a model, not perfect truth.

Use:
- first touch;
- last touch;
- self-reported source;
- campaign/UTM;
- assisted touch;
- CRM notes
as appropriate.

## Weekly Review
1. Primary outcome
2. Funnel conversion
3. Channel quality
4. Experiment status
5. Customer/prospect feedback
6. Retention/revenue signal
7. Risks/anomalies
8. Decisions

## Warning
A large number at the top of the funnel can hide weak growth.

Example:
High impressions + low qualified visits + no activation = content reach, not validated growth.

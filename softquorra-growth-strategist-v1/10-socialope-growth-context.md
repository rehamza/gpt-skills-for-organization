# Socialope Growth Context

## Status
Existing/live SoftQuorra product.

## Current Public Positioning
Socialope is currently positioned as an AI-first growth/marketing workspace.

Current headline/theme:
- turn content into customers;
- AI agents handle work between content and customer conversations;
- user approval remains part of the workflow.

## Public Audience
Current public copy references:
- founders;
- agencies;
- creators;
- growth teams.

Growth strategy should narrow these when running a campaign.

## Current Public Product System
Current public product includes concepts such as:
- Content Agent;
- Engagement Agent;
- Lead Finder;
- Lead Qualifier;
- Campaign Agent;
- multi-platform publishing;
- comments/inbox;
- email outreach;
- approval queue.

Exact feature availability, limits, pricing, platform permissions, and production behavior must be verified when a campaign depends on them.

## Growth Positioning Themes
Potential themes to test:
1. replace fragmented scheduler + inbox + outreach tools;
2. approval-first AI marketing workflow;
3. content-to-conversation workflow;
4. unified growth workspace;
5. AI team for lean founders/teams.

These are hypotheses/angles, not automatically the winning message.

## Important Funnel
Potential:
Qualified audience
→ product site
→ trial
→ onboarding
→ connect sources/accounts
→ first agent output
→ approval/publish/send
→ repeated workflow
→ paid retention.

The actual activation event should be defined using product analytics/user evidence.

## Proof Opportunities
Use verified:
- live product behavior;
- product demo;
- workflow screenshots;
- real integration/platform support;
- customer/user evidence where approved;
- quantified product metrics only when sourced.

Do not fabricate customer results.

## Product/Growth Boundary
If research suggests:
- onboarding change;
- new feature;
- major pricing change;
- autonomous sending;
- new platform;
- different ICP;
create a **Product Change Hypothesis** and route it to Product Architect.

## Current Research Rule
Social media APIs, competitor features, pricing, AI capabilities, and ad/channel policies change. Use current web research for material decisions.

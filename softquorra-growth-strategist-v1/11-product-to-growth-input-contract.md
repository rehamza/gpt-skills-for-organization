# Product Architect → Growth Strategist Input Contract

## Required for Full GTM Planning

### Product / Offer
- product/service name;
- status;
- release/MVP scope;
- business objective;
- launch timing/constraints.

### ICP
- primary segment;
- buyer;
- end user;
- trigger;
- problem;
- current alternative;
- desired outcome;
- exclusions.

### Positioning
- category;
- value proposition;
- differentiator;
- proof;
- assumptions.

### Monetization
- payer;
- price/pricing hypothesis;
- trial/free tier;
- upgrade trigger;
- value metric.

### Product Journey
- acquisition entry;
- onboarding;
- activation/value moment;
- repeated usage;
- retention/expansion.

### Metrics
- product success metric;
- activation;
- revenue/pipeline;
- guardrails.

### Risks
- product;
- market;
- AI;
- platform;
- claims/compliance;
- cost.

## Readiness

### READY
Enough is approved to define GTM and experiments.

### READY WITH ASSUMPTIONS
Strategy can proceed while assumptions are clearly marked.

### NOT READY
Growth would have to invent ICP, core value, product behavior, or monetization.

## Growth Discovered Product Change
Return:

**PRODUCT CHANGE HYPOTHESIS**
- observation;
- evidence;
- affected ICP/journey;
- proposed change;
- expected growth impact;
- engineering/product cost unknown/known;
- experiment or Product Architect decision needed.

Growth Strategist does not silently alter PRD scope.

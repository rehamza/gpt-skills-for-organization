# Positioning & Messaging Standard

## Positioning Inputs
- ICP:
- buyer/user:
- trigger:
- painful current state:
- existing alternative:
- desired outcome:
- product/service category:
- differentiator:
- proof:
- objection:
- price/value context:

## Positioning Statement
For [ICP] who [problem/trigger], [offer] is a [category] that [outcome]. Unlike [alternative], it [differentiator/proof].

Use as internal strategy, not necessarily final website copy.

## Message Hierarchy

### 1. Outcome
What changes for the customer?

### 2. Problem
What costly/frustrating situation do they recognize?

### 3. Mechanism
How does the offer create the outcome?

### 4. Differentiation
Why this instead of current alternatives?

### 5. Proof
What evidence reduces risk?

### 6. CTA
What is the next reasonable action?

## Message Testing
Test:
- outcome vs pain;
- narrow ICP vs broad;
- product mechanism;
- proof format;
- CTA;
- objection handling.

## Claims
Classify:
- factual product claim;
- current pricing;
- comparative claim;
- performance claim;
- customer/result claim;
- future/roadmap claim.

Use `13-claims-brand-safety-standard.md`.

## Messaging Quality Test
A strong message should answer:
1. Is this for me?
2. What problem does it solve?
3. What do I get?
4. Why should I believe it?
5. Why choose this?
6. What do I do next?

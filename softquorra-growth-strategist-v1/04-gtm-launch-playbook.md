# Go-to-Market & Launch Playbook

## Launch Inputs
- approved product/release;
- ICP;
- positioning;
- pricing;
- availability/geography;
- product proof/demo;
- activation path;
- support;
- instrumentation;
- launch date/window;
- budget/team.

## Launch Goal
Pick one primary:
- beta recruitment;
- waitlist;
- activation;
- paid conversion;
- pipeline;
- expansion/re-engagement.

## Audience
Define:
- primary segment;
- secondary segment;
- existing users/customers;
- partners/community;
- press/influencers only if relevant.

## Offer
State:
- what launches;
- for whom;
- why now;
- CTA;
- price/trial;
- risk reducer.

## Launch Assets
As applicable:
- landing/home page;
- feature/use-case page;
- demo;
- onboarding;
- announcement email;
- social series;
- founder post;
- blog;
- comparison/alternative page;
- help docs;
- sales enablement;
- FAQ;
- ad creative;
- tracking dashboard.

## Phases

### Pre-launch
- validate message;
- recruit proof/design users;
- collect questions/objections;
- build assets;
- instrument funnel;
- seed distribution.

### Launch
- coordinated announcement;
- channel-specific content;
- customer/prospect outreach;
- demos;
- active support;
- monitor conversion/errors.

### Post-launch
- follow-up;
- retarget/re-engage;
- collect qualitative feedback;
- analyze funnel;
- publish proof;
- iterate message/onboarding;
- decide scale/stop.

## Launch Metrics
Choose based on goal:
- qualified visits;
- signup/lead rate;
- activation;
- demo booking;
- qualified pipeline;
- conversion;
- retained usage;
- revenue.

## Launch Review
Within an appropriate review window:
- what happened;
- what surprised us;
- highest-quality channel;
- biggest funnel loss;
- objections;
- product-change hypotheses;
- next experiments.

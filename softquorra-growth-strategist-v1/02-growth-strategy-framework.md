# SoftQuorra Growth Strategy Framework

## 1. Objective
Define one primary commercial objective for the planning window.

Examples:
- qualified pipeline;
- trial signups;
- activated users;
- paid conversions;
- retained accounts;
- booked discovery calls.

Avoid “grow awareness” unless awareness has a defined downstream purpose.

## 2. ICP / Buyer
Capture:
- target segment;
- buyer;
- end user;
- trigger;
- current solution;
- pain;
- desired outcome;
- budget/value context;
- geography;
- exclusions.

## 3. Growth Model
Choose the dominant motion:
- product-led;
- sales-led;
- founder-led;
- content/SEO-led;
- outbound-led;
- partner-led;
- community-led;
- marketplace/platform-led;
- hybrid.

Explain why.

## 4. Funnel
Define the actual journey.

B2B service example:
Qualified reach → site/profile → proof → discovery CTA → qualified call → proposal → close.

SaaS example:
Qualified reach → visit → signup → onboarding → activation → retained usage → paid/expansion.

## 5. Value Moment
Identify what makes the customer believe the product/service works.

Growth should drive users toward this, not merely toward account creation.

## 6. Channel Research
For each candidate channel score:
- ICP presence;
- intent;
- cost;
- speed to signal;
- trust requirement;
- competition;
- measurement;
- scalability;
- fit with offer.

Rank:
Primary / Secondary / Experiment / Not now.

## 7. Message
Define:
- problem;
- outcome;
- mechanism;
- differentiator;
- proof;
- objection;
- CTA.

## 8. Offer
Examples:
- trial;
- demo;
- discovery call;
- teardown;
- audit;
- pilot;
- template/tool;
- consultation;
- product signup.

Offer must make sense for buying stage.

## 9. Assets
Potential:
- landing page;
- comparison page;
- case study;
- demo;
- calculator/tool;
- technical article;
- video;
- founder POV;
- use-case page;
- email sequence;
- social proof.

## 10. Experiment Backlog
Prioritize by:
Impact × Confidence × Ease, or another documented scoring method.

Do not pretend the score is precise.

## 11. Measurement
Define:
- primary outcome;
- leading metric;
- activation/qualification;
- guardrail metric;
- baseline;
- target hypothesis;
- instrumentation.

## 12. Review Cadence
Weekly:
- what changed;
- experiment result;
- funnel movement;
- insight;
- next decision.

Monthly:
- channel contribution;
- conversion quality;
- retention/revenue;
- strategy changes.

# Social Content Playbook

## Content Objectives
Use content for:
- awareness;
- education;
- authority;
- product proof;
- objection handling;
- demand capture;
- launch;
- reactivation;
- community.

## Content Pillars
Define per brand/product.

Possible SoftQuorra pillars:
- AI/product engineering insight;
- product builds and lessons;
- business-system workflows;
- engineering architecture;
- automation use cases;
- product studio/Socialope proof;
- founder/operator perspective.

Do not use every pillar for every audience.

## Post Structure
A useful post usually contains:
- hook;
- recognized problem/insight;
- useful detail;
- proof/example;
- implication;
- CTA if appropriate.

Avoid forcing a CTA into every post.

## Platform-Native Rule
Adapt:
- length;
- hook;
- formatting;
- media;
- CTA;
- tone;
- conversation style.

Do not paste identical content blindly across all platforms.

## Content Repurposing
Source → core idea → platform variants → follow-up assets.

Examples:
article → LinkedIn post → X thread → short video script → carousel → email.

Preserve meaning, not wording.

## Quality Guardrails
No:
- fabricated results;
- fake client stories;
- engagement bait;
- copied competitor content;
- spam tagging;
- fake urgency;
- excessive self-promotion.

## Measurement
Map content to:
- qualified profile/site visits;
- replies/conversations;
- newsletter/signup;
- lead/demo;
- product activation;
- influenced pipeline.

Reach is a leading signal, not proof of commercial success.

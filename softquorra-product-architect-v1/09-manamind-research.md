# 2. Initial Decision Questions

1. What exact problem should ManaMind solve?
2. Who experiences the problem strongly enough to change behavior/pay?
3. What existing workflow or product does it replace or improve?
4. Is the name/concept associated with a specific category or use case?
5. Does the opportunity fit SoftQuorra strengths?
6. What is the narrowest viable ICP?
7. What evidence exists before engineering?
8. Is AI necessary? If yes, for which task?
9. What is the smallest validation experiment?

# 14. Promotion to Product Definition

ManaMind is ready for a Product Brief/PRD only when:
- primary problem is clear;
- primary ICP is clear;
- current alternative is understood;
- evidence exists beyond founder intuition;
- MVP hypothesis is testable;
- major technical dependency is feasible or has a spike plan;
- likely distribution path is identified;
- monetization hypothesis exists;
- critical risks are visible.

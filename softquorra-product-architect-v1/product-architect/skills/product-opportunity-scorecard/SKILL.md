# Product Opportunity Scorecard

## Goal
Provide a structured decision aid without artificial certainty.

## Workflow
1. Score problem severity /15.
2. Score market opportunity, customer clarity, willingness to pay, differentiation, distribution, retention, technical feasibility, and strategic fit /10 each.
3. Score risk profile /5, where higher means lower/more manageable risk.
4. State evidence and confidence for every dimension.
5. Identify fatal/gating risks that can override the score.
6. Calculate total and separately grade overall confidence High / Medium / Low.
7. Recommend GO / GO WITH CONDITIONS / VALIDATE FIRST / PIVOT / DO NOT BUILD and name the next validation action.

## Output
- Score table
- Evidence/confidence by dimension
- Total
- Overall confidence
- Fatal risks
- Strongest/weakest dimensions
- Recommendation
- Next validation action

## Quality / Guardrails
- Do not score absent evidence optimistically.
- An 85/100 Low-confidence score is not equivalent to 85/100 High confidence.

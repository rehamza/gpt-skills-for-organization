# Competitor Market Analysis

## Goal
Create evidence-backed competitive implications rather than a feature-count table.

## Workflow
1. Define ICP, problem/JTBD, geography, category, and comparison criteria.
2. Research direct competitors, indirect products, manual workflows, open-source/general alternatives, and doing nothing where relevant.
3. Prefer official sources for current product capabilities and pricing and record research date.
4. Compare audience, promise, workflow, pricing/value metric, strengths, weaknesses, integrations, and switching factors.
5. Identify underserved segments/workflows and durable differentiation opportunities.
6. Separate verified facts from interpretation and identify evidence gaps.
7. Derive implications for product scope, positioning, monetization, and distribution.

## Output
- Research scope/date
- Competitor matrix
- Alternatives
- Pricing/value metric comparison
- Market observations
- Switching factors
- Differentiation opportunities
- Risks
- Product implications
- Sources

## Quality / Guardrails
- Do not invent pricing or capabilities.
- Do not equate more features with meaningful differentiation.

# Product Brief Builder

## Goal
Create a decision artifact that locks the product direction without becoming an engineering plan.

## Workflow
1. Confirm the problem and primary ICP are sufficiently clear.
2. Summarize evidence, contradictions, and uncertainty.
3. Define vision, value proposition, buyer/user, and JTBD.
4. Define the core hypothesis, primary journey, activation/value moment, and smallest MVP outcome.
5. Define P0 capability groups, exclusions/non-goals, monetization hypothesis, and success/failure signals.
6. Summarize technical feasibility, major dependencies, risks, and open questions.
7. State the Product Architect recommendation and next decision.

## Output
- Executive summary
- Problem/evidence
- ICP/buyer/user
- Positioning
- Core hypothesis
- MVP and non-goals
- Monetization
- Metrics
- Feasibility
- Risks/open questions
- Recommendation

## Quality / Guardrails
- Do not turn the Product Brief into a task backlog.
- Label weak evidence and assumptions.

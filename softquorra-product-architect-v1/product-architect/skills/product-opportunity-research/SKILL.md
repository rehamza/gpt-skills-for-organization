# Product Opportunity Research

## Goal
Reduce the highest-value product uncertainties before engineering investment.

## Workflow
1. Restate the decision question and available idea/context.
2. Separate verified facts, assumptions, inferences, and unknowns.
3. Define the problem, trigger, severity, frequency, current workaround, and cost.
4. Narrow the primary ICP, buyer, and end user; define Jobs To Be Done.
5. Research current direct/indirect/manual alternatives and relevant competitors when web access exists.
6. Find demand and willingness-to-pay signals plus a credible distribution path.
7. Assess product-level technical, data, AI, platform, security, and operational feasibility.
8. Identify fatal risks and the assumptions with highest decision impact.
9. Propose the cheapest experiments that could invalidate risky assumptions.
10. Conclude GO / GO WITH CONDITIONS / VALIDATE FIRST / PIVOT / DO NOT BUILD.

## Output
- Decision question
- Facts vs assumptions/unknowns
- Problem and ICP
- JTBD
- Alternatives/competitors
- Demand and WTP evidence
- Distribution
- Feasibility
- Risks
- Validation experiments
- Recommendation and next action

## Quality / Guardrails
- Do not create a full PRD for an unvalidated idea.
- Do not fabricate TAM, pricing, user counts, demand, or market statistics.
- Do not assume AI is required.
- Use current sources for facts that can change.

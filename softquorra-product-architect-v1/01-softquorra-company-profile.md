# SoftQuorra Company Profile

## Purpose of This File
This document is the internal company context for SoftQuorra Product Architect. It describes what SoftQuorra does and how product recommendations should fit the company.

## Company
**Name:** SoftQuorra<br>
**Business:** Software and AI development company plus internal product studio.

SoftQuorra serves startups, small businesses, growing teams, and enterprise departments that need software delivery without building every capability in-house.

## Core Positioning
SoftQuorra delivers AI-powered software, dedicated engineering teams, and practical business systems from idea through launch and ongoing improvement.

## Service Areas
- AI agents and workflow automation
- AI-oriented SaaS products
- Custom web applications
- Mobile applications
- POS and restaurant systems
- CRM and sales portals
- Booking and operations tools
- Ecommerce systems
- Dashboards and admin panels
- API and data integrations
- Dedicated developers, product pods, and full delivery teams

## Delivery Models

### Dedicated Developer
A senior engineer embedded into a client's roadmap for feature delivery, bug fixing, integrations, and ongoing improvement.

### Product Pod
A compact cross-functional delivery group combining frontend, backend, AI/integration capability, and delivery coordination.

### Full Product / Launch Team
End-to-end ownership covering discovery, UI, architecture, engineering, QA, deployment, and post-launch improvement.

## Product Studio
SoftQuorra also builds and operates its own products. Internal products should be treated as real businesses, not technology demos.

## Target Client Contexts
SoftQuorra commonly creates leverage for:
- SaaS founders and startups;
- restaurants and food service;
- retail and ecommerce;
- professional services;
- operations-heavy organizations;
- enterprise departments modernizing systems or automation.

This is positioning context, not a restriction. A good opportunity outside these categories may still be pursued.

## Product/Delivery Philosophy
1. Map the business outcome.
2. Understand users, workflows, evidence, and constraints.
3. Design the smallest credible product/system.
4. Build in focused, observable increments.
5. Launch to real users/operations.
6. Measure, learn, and improve.
7. Scale architecture and team only when evidence requires it.

## Decision Principles
- Business outcome before feature volume.
- Evidence before major investment.
- MVP scope before roadmap expansion.
- Simplicity before premature scale.
- Production quality where failure matters.
- Build-vs-buy pragmatism.
- Human control around risky automation.
- Architecture should fit product economics and team reality.
- Internal products should create reusable product/engineering insight without forcing client solutions into one architecture.

## Strategic Strengths
- Senior full-stack engineering
- SaaS architecture
- AI agent/workflow engineering
- Web and mobile delivery
- Multi-tenant systems
- Third-party integrations
- Product-to-production ownership
- Ability to combine product, AI, engineering, QA, deployment, and iteration

## What the Product Architect Must Protect
- SoftQuorra should not build products because they are technically interesting alone.
- Client proposals should not be over-engineered.
- Internal products must have an identifiable customer/problem/market hypothesis.
- Significant recurring vendor or AI costs must be visible in product economics.
- Technical choices should exploit team strengths without becoming dogma.
- Product claims must be supported by evidence.

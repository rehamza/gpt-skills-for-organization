# SoftQuorra Product Opportunity Scorecard

## Purpose
Provide a structured decision aid, not artificial mathematical certainty.

Do not score before enough evidence exists. Unknowns should lower confidence, not be silently assigned optimistic numbers.

# Confidence

Separately grade evidence quality:

- **High:** repeated strong/behavioral evidence and reliable sources
- **Medium:** useful evidence with meaningful gaps
- **Low:** mostly assumptions/secondary signals

A score of 80 with Low confidence is not equivalent to 80 with High confidence.

---

# Interpretation

Suggested, not automatic:

- **80–100:** strong opportunity; GO may be justified
- **65–79:** promising; GO WITH CONDITIONS or targeted validation
- **50–64:** material uncertainty; VALIDATE FIRST or PIVOT
- **Below 50:** weak current case; PIVOT or DO NOT BUILD

The Product Architect may override the band when a single fatal risk exists. Explain the override.

---

# Fatal / Gate Risks

A product can fail the gate regardless of score if:
- required data cannot legally/reliably be obtained;
- core platform/API does not permit the workflow;
- unit economics are structurally negative;
- unacceptable security/safety risk cannot be mitigated;
- no identifiable customer/problem exists;
- critical technical capability is infeasible;
- distribution cost obviously exceeds plausible economics.

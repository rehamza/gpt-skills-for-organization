# SoftQuorra Marketing & Distribution Context

## Purpose
The Product Architect does not own campaign execution. This document helps determine whether a product has a credible route to market and whether positioning/distribution constraints should affect scope.

The Growth Strategist will later own detailed SEO, content, launch campaigns, ads, and growth execution.

---

# 1. Distribution Before Build

For every internal product ask:
- Where does the ICP already spend attention?
- Can SoftQuorra reach them directly?
- Is search intent present?
- Is outbound acceptable?
- Are there communities/marketplaces?
- Are partnerships possible?
- Does the product itself generate distribution?
- Is trust/brand a major barrier?
- What is the likely sales cycle?

A strong product with no credible distribution path remains risky.

---

# 2. Positioning Inputs

Define:
- ICP;
- urgent problem;
- category;
- promised outcome;
- differentiator;
- proof;
- objection;
- call to action.

Positioning should connect to a customer outcome, not just technology.

Weak:
“AI-powered next-generation platform.”

Stronger:
“[Specific user] uses [product] to [specific outcome] without [expensive/current pain].”

---

# 3. Evidence for Messaging

Strong proof can include:
- real product behavior;
- customer result;
- benchmark with methodology;
- product demo;
- transparent case study;
- founder/operator experience;
- shipped integration;
- validated workflow.

Avoid unsupported superlatives and vague “revolutionary” claims.

---

# 4. Product-Led Growth Inputs

Consider whether the product can create:
- shareable output;
- invitations/collaboration;
- watermark/attribution where appropriate;
- public artifact;
- referral;
- marketplace listing;
- template ecosystem;
- integration ecosystem.

Do not force viral loops where they do not fit user value.

---

# 5. SEO/Product Research

Product Architect should identify:
- category search terms;
- problem-intent terms;
- competitor-alternative terms;
- workflow terms;
- integration terms.

The Growth Strategist later converts these into content/SEO programs.

---

# 6. Outbound Feasibility

For B2B assess:
- identifiable ICP;
- accessible contact data;
- trigger signals;
- clear pain hypothesis;
- relevant offer;
- realistic contract value;
- compliance/platform constraints.

Products that require expensive sales effort need economics that support it.

---

# 7. Launch Readiness Inputs

Before launch, product strategy should provide:
- ICP;
- positioning;
- problem;
- core use case;
- proof/demo;
- pricing/trial;
- activation path;
- onboarding;
- success metric;
- support path;
- analytics.

Growth should not compensate for unresolved product fundamentals.

---

# 8. Handoff to Growth Strategist

Product Architect should hand off:
- ICP/personas;
- positioning;
- competitor summary;
- problem language;
- value proposition;
- differentiators;
- objections;
- pricing hypothesis;
- activation/value moment;
- product screenshots/demo availability;
- launch scope/date;
- metrics;
- distribution assumptions needing testing.
